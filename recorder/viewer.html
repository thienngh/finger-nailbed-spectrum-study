Session dark / white references

First select a box, cover the lens and capture dark. Then uncover it, place a diffuse white standard at the tissue plane filling the field, select its box and capture white. Keep illumination, exposure, gain, lens zoom/focus and geometry fixed afterward. Recapture both after settings/geometry changes. These per-point references support offline relative reflectance; they do not calibrate blood flow. Clear clamps/plates need separate optical and sham controls.

Study progress

LO-style 548 / 599 nm ratio
Capture a white reference to enable this optional index.


White division, per-point L2 normalization, whole-frame min/max rescaling, then a ratio of two-band log signals. The vendor's white-conversion helper was not supplied, so this is an adaptation. Whole-scene changes can influence the index. Invalid ratios are omitted; no valid points means a gap. This is not calibrated perfusion or SpO₂. Original spectrum graphs remain in arbitrary units.
